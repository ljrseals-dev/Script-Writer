INT. COFFEE SHOP - DAY

Rain streaks the windows. A SCREENWRITER sits alone, a blank page glowing
in front of her. She types one word, deletes it, and sighs.

                    SCREENWRITER
          (to herself)
     Just start with the coffee.

The BARISTA slides a cup across the counter without looking up.

                    BARISTA
     On the house. You look like
     you need it.

The screenwriter laughs, once, and starts typing.

CUT TO:

EXT. STREET - CONTINUOUS

She walks home in the rain, notebook clutched to her chest.
