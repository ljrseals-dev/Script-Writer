## 4. Indemnification

### 4.1 Licensee indemnity — production-related Third-Party Claims

To the extent permitted by applicable law, Licensee shall defend and indemnify Provider and its officers, directors, employees, and agents (the **“Provider Indemnitees”**) against Losses from a Covered Claim brought by a third party, but only to the extent the Claim is caused by:

(a) Licensee's negligent or wrongful conduct in planning, conducting, or controlling Production Activities;

(b) Licensee's material breach of Section 3 or failure to obtain a required permit, provide required training, or maintain safe conditions, where that breach or failure caused the Claim;

(c) Licensee's use of the Software or Output in physical production in a manner not authorized by the underlying agreement or contrary to the express safety limitations in Section 3; or

(d) tangible property damage or bodily injury caused by Licensee's Production Activities.

This indemnity does not apply to the extent a Claim is caused by the negligence, breach, or wrongful conduct of a Provider Indemnitee, or where indemnification is prohibited by applicable law. It does not require Licensee to indemnify a Provider Indemnitee for that indemnitee's own negligence, gross negligence, willful misconduct, fraud, or non-delegable legal duty where such shifting is prohibited. Any allocation shall reflect comparative responsibility to the extent recognized by governing law.

### 4.2 Provider indemnity — specified intellectual-property claims

Subject to Sections 4.3 and 5, Provider shall defend Licensee against a Covered Claim alleging that the unmodified Software, as supplied by Provider and used by Licensee within the scope of the underlying agreement and documentation, directly infringes a third party's copyright, patent, or trademark, and shall pay Losses finally awarded or settlement amounts approved under Section 5.

This is a specific third-party IP indemnity. It is not an indemnity for a direct warranty dispute between the parties, and it does not convert the warranty statements in Section 2 into an unlimited guarantee.

### 4.3 Provider indemnity exclusions and response

Provider has no obligation under Section 4.2 to the extent a claim arises from: (a) Output, prompts, scripts, or other materials created or supplied by Licensee or a third party; (b) Licensee's modification of the Software; (c) combination with items not supplied or approved by Provider, if the claim would not have arisen without that combination; (d) use outside the license scope or contrary to documentation, if the claim would not have arisen from compliant use; (e) continued use after Provider supplies a non-infringing replacement or workaround that does not materially reduce core functionality; or (f) open-source or other third-party components, but only to the extent the applicable third-party license or law prevents Provider from providing the indemnity. Provider must identify excluded third-party components and applicable license terms in a schedule; a blanket exclusion for all third-party components is not intended.

If the Software becomes, or Provider reasonably believes it is likely to become, subject to an infringement claim, Provider may at its expense: (i) obtain the right for Licensee to continue using the affected Software; (ii) modify or replace it with a materially equivalent non-infringing alternative; or (iii) terminate the affected license and refund prepaid, unused license fees for the affected Software, prorated from termination, unless the underlying agreement specifies a different negotiated remedy. The selected remedy does not eliminate liability for Losses already covered under Section 4.2.

### 4.4 No general negligence indemnity

Except for the express allocations in Section 4.1 and Section 4.2, neither party assumes the other party's liabilities. Each party remains responsible for its own acts, omissions, negligence, gross negligence, willful misconduct, fraud, and statutory obligations to the extent required by law. No general indemnity for a party's own conduct is created by this Addendum.

## 5. Indemnification Procedure

5.1 The party seeking indemnification (the **“Indemnified Party”**) shall give the other party (the **“Indemnifying Party”**) prompt written notice of a Covered Claim, with reasonable details then available. A delay relieves the Indemnifying Party only to the extent it proves material prejudice caused by the delay.

5.2 The Indemnifying Party may assume control of the defense only after it: (a) confirms in writing that the Claim is within the stated indemnity, subject to any expressly reserved allocation; (b) appoints competent counsel reasonably acceptable to the Indemnified Party; and (c) confirms it will diligently conduct and fund the covered defense. If coverage or allocation is disputed, the parties shall cooperate on a reasonable interim defense arrangement without waiving their positions.

5.3 The Indemnified Party shall provide reasonable cooperation at the Indemnifying Party's expense. It may participate through separate counsel at its own expense, except that the Indemnifying Party shall pay reasonable separate-counsel costs where a material conflict exists, the Indemnifying Party fails to defend diligently, or applicable law requires.

5.4 The Indemnifying Party shall not settle a Claim without the Indemnified Party's prior written consent if the settlement: (a) admits fault or wrongdoing by the Indemnified Party; (b) imposes non-monetary obligations, restrictions, or continuing duties on it; (c) does not provide an unconditional release of it from the settled Claim; or (d) otherwise materially prejudices it. Consent shall not be unreasonably withheld or delayed. A settlement solely for money, fully funded by the Indemnifying Party, with a complete release and no admission or continuing obligation for the Indemnified Party, may be made with its consent, not to be unreasonably withheld.

5.5 No party may admit liability, waive defenses, or incur obligations on behalf of another party without written authorization.

5.6 The duty to defend, where expressly provided, is separate from the duty to pay Losses. It applies to the defense of allegations within the scope of the applicable indemnity, subject to allocation and governing law; it is not conditioned on waiting for a final judgment. Payment of indemnified Losses is limited to amounts covered by the applicable indemnity and determined by final judgment or approved settlement.
